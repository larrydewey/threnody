- Group calls: voice and video for up to eight people. A member who can't reach another directly is relayed by a third, who can't see the media.
- Calls ring all your contact's devices; the first to answer takes it.
- Car audio and headset buttons work, and a phone call puts a Threnody call on hold.
- Security fixes: contacts are encrypted, and file names don't show who you talk to.
- Cover traffic now also hides relay traffic, using more data.
- Downgrading isn't supported, but loses nothing.
